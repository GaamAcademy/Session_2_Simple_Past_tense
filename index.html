Test your comprehensive grasp of the English Past Simple tense: completed actions, finished time markers, regular -ed spelling & phonetic pronunciations, was/were without auxiliary did, the invariant bare infinitive rule, storytelling sequences, unreal conditions (second conditional / wish), and polite social distancing.

Regular suffixes (-ed, -ied, doubled consonants), irregular changes, and /t/, /d/, /ɪd/ phonetics (A1–B1).

2. The Verb 'Be'

3. Auxiliary Mechanics

4. Time & Sequences

Finished time expressions (yesterday, in 2003, ago) and narrative event chains.

5. Past vs Present Perfect

Identifying finished past periods vs. unspecified life experience and ongoing results.

6. Unreal & Politeness

Second conditional (If I won...), wish, and polite present requests (B1–B2).
